About Yizkor Books

Yizkor books are memorial books commemorating a Jewish community destroyed during the Holocaust. Published by former residents as remembrances of homes, people, and ways of life lost during World War II.

Most books are written in Yiddish or Hebrew. Since the 1990s, many have been digitized and made available online. This site makes scanned pages of these books digitally searchable.

Instructions & Known Issues

How to Use

  1. Pick a Yizkor Book from the drop-down above.
  2. Enter a search term in the book's language (Hebrew or Yiddish).
  3. Click the Page number to open the original scan in a new tab.
  4. Follow the line and word numbers to locate your term within the scan.

Known Issues

  • Only exact and contains matches are currently supported.
  • Inconsistent line numbering due to variable book layouts.
  • Report issues on GitHub or via the feedback form.

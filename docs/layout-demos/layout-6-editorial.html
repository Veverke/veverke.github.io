About Yizkor Books

Yizkor books are memorial books commemorating a Jewish community destroyed during the Holocaust. Published by former residents as remembrances of homes, people, and ways of life lost during World War II.

Most books are written in Yiddish or Hebrew. Since the 1990s, many have been digitized and made available online. This site combines digitization software to make their scanned pages searchable with page, line, and word precision.

Instructions & Known Issues

How to Use

  1. Click a book card above to select it.
  2. Enter a search term in the book's language (Hebrew or Yiddish).
  3. Click the green Page column to open the original scan in a new tab.
  4. Use the line and word numbers to locate your term in the scan.

Known Issues

  • Only exact and contains matches are currently supported.
  • Inconsistent line numbering due to variable page layouts.
  • Report issues on GitHub or via the feedback form.
